About Coding Club

Coding Club was founded by a group of students who wanted a fun and supportive space to learn programming together. We welcome members of all experience levels, from complete beginners to those who already know how to code. Every week, we host workshops, coding challenges, and project-based sessions where members can build real skills and real projects. Beyond the technical side, we also aim to build a community where students can make friends, find mentors, and grow their confidence in tech.
